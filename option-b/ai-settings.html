Guardrails

Built in before any feature is switched on

  • AI labels on all output

    Every AI draft, suggestion and answer shows an “AI generated” label to staff and students.

    Always on · cannot be switched off

  • Audit log of every AI call

    RTO, user, role, feature, time, model version, a hash of prompt and output, and the human decision. Append-only.

    Always on · cannot be switched off

  • Australian processing

    Inference and any temporary prompt storage stay in an Australian region. No training on RTO data.

    Always on · cannot be switched off

  • Data kept per RTO

    Prompts, retrieval and memory never cross from one RTO to another. Users only see what they already can.

    Always on · cannot be switched off

  • No AI final grade

    AI can suggest. A qualified assessor accepts, edits or rejects, and records the decision.

    Always on · cannot be switched off

  • No auto-issued certificates

    AI can never issue a certificate or statement of attainment, or close an RPL decision.

    Always on · cannot be switched off
